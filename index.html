Estimated fuel
—
Set your fuel level to start.

Defaults: 2010 F-150 SVT Raptor, 26 gal tank. E85 MPG is a starting guess. Track a couple of fill-ups (miles ÷ gallons) and put your real number here, since it sets every range calculation.

Reserve is the minimum % the planner will let you arrive with. Max detour is how far off the route a station can be.

API key: station data comes from the U.S. DOE Alternative Fuels Data Center. DEMO_KEY is rate-limited, so get a free key in a minute at developer.nlr.gov/signup.

Routing: OSRM · Geocoding: OpenStreetMap Nominatim · Map © OpenStreetMap, © CARTO. Station listings can be out of date, so call ahead on long empty stretches.
